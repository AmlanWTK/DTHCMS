| **"Renally cleared" was widened to "prescribing depends on knowing the kidney function" (CP79).** The plan's words were the first; implemented literally that flag is wrong in both directions for drugs this clinic uses daily. Empagliflozin is *not* renally cleared and still must not be started without an eGFR; linagliptin *is* biliary and is the alternative every renal rule offers, so a clearance flag would have fired on it and taught him to ignore the column. Confirm or reject the widening. | Dr. Nahid | CP79 closing |
| **Dialysis is out of scope and nothing models it (CP79).** A patient on haemodialysis reads exactly as any other patient in the renal indicator. That is deliberate — an eGFR on dialysis describes residual function, the dosing is driven by the schedule and by dialysability, and banding it would produce confident wrong answers — but it is a limitation to know about rather than a behaviour to rely on. In scope or not is his call. | Dr. Nahid | CP79 closing |
| **Correction after dispensing (CP80).** The plan left this open; it is now decided and built. A correction is always permitted from SIGNED, PRINTED or DISPENSED; it creates a new prescription that supersedes the original; the original is never edited and never withdrawn from history; and when it had already been dispensed the correction records that fact **on itself**, so a reader knows medicine has left the counter. **What the pharmacy then does — whether the patient is called back — is a clinical and operational decision this system does not make.** That half is his. | Dr. Nahid | CP80 closing |
| **Cancelling stops at PRINTED (CP80).** A prescription can be cancelled from DRAFT, QA_REVIEW or SIGNED. Once printed, the paper is in the patient's hand and the pharmacy may act on it, so the only route is a correction — the record shows a replacement rather than an absence. This is the one clinical judgement in the transition matrix and it is a proposal. | Dr. Nahid | CP80 closing |
| **Ten real prescriptions, timed against paper.** CP81's own manual verification, and the first checkpoint at which it is possible: the aggregate and the API exist, the editor does not. Worth agreeing the paper baseline before the editor is built rather than after. | Dr. Nahid + Amlan | CP81 |
