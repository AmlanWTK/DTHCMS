# Access matrix

_Generated by `go run ./tools/accessmatrix` from `internal/rbac`. Do not edit; a test compares this file with the engine._

Every role against every permission (CP19). A cell is the decision for a person wearing that
role as their active hat, acting on a resource at their own station in their own facility —
the most favourable case, so what is denied here is denied everywhere. `any`, `station` and
`own` are the reach of an allow; a dash is a permission the role does not hold; a named rule
is an explicit deny from blueprint §4.4, which would win even if the role held the permission.

Read it the way the blueprint is written: one paragraph per role.

## The rules

| Rule | Applies to | Says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `nutritionist_no_prescriptions` | NUTRITIONIST, every `prescription.*` | blueprint §4.4: the nutritionist has no access to prescriptions |
| `pharmacist_no_diagnoses` | PHARMACIST, every `diagnosis.*` and every sensitive permission | blueprint §4.4: the pharmacist sees drugs and dosing only; diagnoses are hidden |
| `registration_blinded` | REGISTRATION, every sensitive permission | blueprint §4.4: registration is blinded to sensitive clinical data |
| `blinded_role_sensitive_resource` | REGISTRATION and PHARMACIST, any read of a resource carrying a diagnosis | the resource carries a diagnosis or clinical interpretation, which this role may not see |
| `field_worker_no_facility_records` | FIELD_WORKER, every `records.*` and `diagnosis.*` | a field worker records outreach captures; clinic records are not theirs to read |

Sensitive permissions — the ones blinding is about: `patient.read.clinical`, `records.read`, `diagnosis.read`, `diagnosis.write`, `ai.synthesis.read`, `history.read`, `alert.read`, `alert.acknowledge`.
